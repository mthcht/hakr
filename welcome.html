Welcome to HAKR

Edit any webpage locally. Click text to rewrite it, click images to swap them. No developer tools required.

How it works

01
Open the HAKR popup
Click the HAKR icon in your browser toolbar.
02
Start editing
Press “Start editing” to enable edit mode on the current page. The popup closes automatically.
03
Click anything
Click any text to rewrite it inline. Click any image to replace it with a URL or a file from your computer.
04
Stop or reset
Open the popup again to stop editing, or reset the page to undo all your changes.

Good to know

  • All changes are local. Only you see them - nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
  • Reloading the page restores the original content.
  • HAKR cannot run on browser-internal pages (chrome://, about:, etc.) or extension stores.
⌨
Keyboard shortcut
Press Ctrl+Shift+H (⌘+Shift+H on Mac) on any page to toggle edit mode without opening the popup.
